How It Works

The Art made just for you

01

Inquiry

Tell me about your story, characters, and the mood you're after. References welcome — clear vision or blank slate, both work.

02

Quote & Timeline

I reply within a day or the next day. Then we'll figure it all out together — the details, the timeline, the price, and anything else you're wondering about. Nothing gets skipped.

03

Sketch & Approval

You'll get the first sketch within a week. We'll go back and forth until the composition feels right. Then I'll guide you through line art → flat colours (change any colour you like) → full render (small adjustments only). Just honest progress, step by step, together. The minimum turnaround time is 2 weeks; the maximum is 1 month.

04

Final Artwork

You receive high-resolution files ready to publish, print, or share — your art, your story.

The Artist

Hi, I'm Evgenia

— but you may know me as Pinky Kei. I'm a digital illustrator in love with romantic fantasy and dark romance: the drama, the danger, the impossible longing, the slow burn — and my absolute favourite, enemies to lovers. My work lives in that space between tenderness and darkness, where readers like us feel at home.

I'm self-taught (though I had a few art-school years as a child). I found my way back to drawing only after university, in 2019, when I wanted to bring my own fanfiction characters to life. That spark never went away. Now I help authors do the same: bring their characters and stories into the light.

Every commission is made together with you — hand-made, personal, and true to your world. ✦
